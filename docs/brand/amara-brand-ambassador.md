# Amara — GEO-Pulse brand ambassador

Status: approved fictional brand character for GEO-Pulse-owned creative.

Amara is the Black woman introduced in the latest MSP Instagram post. She is a fictional visual ambassador, not a customer, employee, founder, analyst, or testimonial source. Public copy must never imply that she used GEO-Pulse or achieved a result.

## Identity invariants

- Preserve the same facial structure, skin tone, age range, brown eyes, nose, lips, and calm confident expression.
- Keep natural-looking skin texture and realistic proportions. Avoid beauty-filter retouching and stock-photo gloss.
- Small gold hoop earrings are the recurring accessory.
- Hair may vary among the approved natural styles: short natural curls, side-swept braided bob, shoulder-length twist-out, and high braided ponytail.
- Outfit may vary among tailored black, navy, ivory, emerald, and restrained warm-neutral pieces.
- The emotional register is practical founder-operator confidence: alert, thoughtful, warm, never theatrical.

## Visual system

- Palette: charcoal, warm ivory, antique gold, restrained emerald or navy accents.
- Settings: real offices, worktables, whiteboards, or quiet technical environments. No sci-fi control rooms, glowing AI icons, robots, or fake dashboards.
- Composition: keep Amara inside the central crop-safe area and reserve clean space for one short useful headline.
- Use no generated logos or embedded product claims in the source portrait. GEO-Pulse typography and claims are added deterministically in the renderer.

## Usage rules

- Use Amara in the evergreen MSP education series, selected landing-page/editorial illustrations, and campaign thumbnails.
- Do not use another human avatar in the evergreen series while this system is active.
- Real founder/customer photography remains distinct and must be labeled by its actual role and consent.
- Do not attribute quotes, metrics, endorsements, case studies, or personal stories to Amara.
- Every claim overlaid on an Amara image must satisfy GEO-Pulse claim review: no ranking or citation guarantees, no fabricated proof, and no capability presented as live when it is not.

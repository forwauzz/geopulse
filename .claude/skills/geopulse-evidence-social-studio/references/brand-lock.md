# GEO-Pulse social brand lock

Templates do not define the brand. Choose one approved visual family before opening Canva and record
it in the content brief.

## Canonical identity

- Product name: GEO-Pulse
- Core promise: AI visibility you can prove, fix, and report.
- Default product CTA: Run a free audit.
- Product loop: Measure the gap. Explain the cause. Fix what matters. Verify the result.
- Working wordmark: text only, GEO-Pulse, Newsreader 500, tracked uppercase treatment, dark gold
  #8A6D33; no enclosing shape and no generated icon.
- Display type: Newsreader, Georgia fallback.
- Body, labels, data, and controls: Inter, system sans fallback.

Never write GeoPulse, GEO Pulse, or Geopulse.

## Approved visual families

### editorial_core — default

Use for audit findings, benchmark education, intelligence explanations, product education, carousels,
LinkedIn posts, and most static posts.

- canvas #F8F9F9
- surface #FFFFFF
- soft surface #F1F4F4
- primary ink #2C3435
- secondary ink #586162
- action slate #565E74
- muted gold #B79C60, structural accents only
- dark-gold wordmark #8A6D33
- outline #ABB4B5

Style: calm whitespace, clear evidence hierarchy, restrained decoration, soft borders, and readable
data. Gold is not a full-background luxury treatment and does not own the CTA.

### editorial_dark

Use sparingly for high-contrast proof or intelligence posts.

- background #090D12

- primary text #E6EBEF
- secondary text #9FADBA
- action slate #7D92BF

### amara_education

Use when Amara anchors an evergreen education asset.

- charcoal, warm ivory, antique gold
- restrained navy or emerald only as supporting accents
- real office, worktable, whiteboard, or quiet technical setting
- one short headline with clean negative space

Amara is fictional. Preserve her identity anchors and never imply that she used GEO-Pulse, achieved a
result, or supplied a quote. Use the approved files under public/branding/social/amara/ when
available.

### landing_colorblock

This is a campaign variant, not the default social identity. Use only when the founder or brief
explicitly selects it.

- ink #14181A
- acid lime #C8F04A
- electric blue #2B4CD8
- soft #F4F6F2
- muted #4A5254

Do not mix it with muted-gold editorial styling in one asset.

## Visual invariants

- Use wide, calm whitespace and a clear evidence hierarchy.
- Use one display face and one body face.
- Use semantic blue, green, amber, and red for data meaning, not decoration.
- Use full pills only for compact status or metadata.
- Keep the strongest message and CTA inside feed/reel safe areas.
- Prefer real operators, evidence, reports, measured answer snippets, and practical work contexts.

Avoid:

- inheriting template colours or fonts
- metallic or gradient gold
- neon AI futurism, generic purple gradients, glowing brains, robots, or circuit heads
- fake dashboards, fabricated charts, unsupported customer photos, or testimonial cues
- hard black outlines, heavy shadows, excessive glass, and pill-shaped text everywhere
- a new logo icon generated inside an image

## Conflict rule

When another template, old asset, or skill conflicts with this file, this file wins for social
production. If the founder gives a newer explicit palette decision, record it in the brief and update
this file rather than silently improvising.

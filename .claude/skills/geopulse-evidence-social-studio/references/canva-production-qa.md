# Canva production and visual QA

Use Canva templates as motion/layout scaffolds. Never use their copy, palette, type, people, charts, or
claims as evidence.

## Before Canva

Require:

- validated evidence brief
- five-line audience brief
- one content lane
- one CTA
- selected visual family from brand-lock.md
- final on-screen copy with claim IDs

If any is missing, do not edit the template.

## Template selection

Select for:

- correct format
- enough scenes/slides for the real word count
- clean hierarchy
- rebrandable structure
- readable motion

Reject:

- a layout whose impact depends on unapproved colours or type
- more than two typefaces
- script-dominant templates
- decorative collage that competes with evidence
- unsafe text areas
- a scene count that forces filler or blank gaps

The optional sibling geopulse-canva-reel-finder may shortlist public templates. Its output is a
starting point, not approval.

## Rebrand order

1. Set the exact visual family palette.
2. Replace every wordmark with the canonical text wordmark.
3. Replace display/body fonts with the approved pair.
4. Replace generic people with approved Amara assets or relevant non-testimonial imagery.
5. Replace all template copy with final approved copy.
6. Rebuild charts from evidence; never relabel decorative chart shapes as real data.
7. Add the single CTA and getgeopulse.com or the approved link-in-bio instruction.
8. Remove decorative AI imagery and unused template elements.

## Reel timing

A useful default:

- 0.0–1.5s: hook
- 1.5–5.0s: evidence
- 5.0–9.0s: why it matters
- 9.0–13.0s: what to do
- final 2–3s: CTA

Adapt to the idea, but:

- never leave blank frames between scenes
- do not make text enter after the viewer has time to read the rest
- keep list-item timing intentional
- keep the CTA readable for at least 2 seconds
- do not hide key copy behind the Instagram caption/profile/control zones

## Safe-area and legibility checks

- Inspect the 9:16 export, not only Canva’s editor.
- Keep essential Reel copy away from the top and bottom UI zones and the right-side controls.
- Keep static/carousel essentials inside the central feed-safe area.
- Use high contrast and readable text sizes.
- Limit text density; move method detail into the caption.
- Watch every video from the first frame through the last.
- Inspect every carousel slide at phone size.

## Claim QA

For every number, chart, ranking word, comparison, or model/provider name:

- locate the evidence-brief claim_id
- compare the exact wording and qualifier
- verify date, denominator, cohort, and model lane
- confirm observation and interpretation remain separate
- confirm the graphic does not imply causality or guaranteed performance

If a chart cannot be reconstructed from the evidence, remove it.

## Brand QA

- correct GEO-Pulse spelling
- no generated logo icon
- approved palette only
- Newsreader display and Inter body, unless the selected approved family says otherwise
- gold used structurally, not as luxury decoration
- Amara identity and role preserved
- no inherited template colours, fonts, hashtags, usernames, URLs, or stock testimonial cues

## Release result

Return PASS, REVISE, or BLOCK.

- PASS: evidence, copy, brand, timing, safe areas, CTA, and export all pass.
- REVISE: bounded visual/copy changes can resolve the issue without changing the claim.
- BLOCK: evidence is missing/private/ineligible, the claim overreaches, the template cannot preserve
  legibility, or the export cannot be validated.

Never download, schedule, or publish a REVISE or BLOCK asset.
